## Recheck of historical arithmetic, before production additions

The old one-CX23 estimate gives **$7.09/month** for compute plus one IPv4. Two CX23s with one public IPv4 give **$13.58/month**. If Hetzner automatic backups cover each VM, add **$1.30** or **$2.60** respectively, yielding **$8.39** and **$16.18** before domain, off-provider database backups, email, monitoring, tax, and any additional storage. These are arithmetic examples only: two VMs are still single-instance app and database components, and neither sketch proves acceptable production reliability. [Server price schedule](https://docs.hetzner.com/general/infrastructure-and-availability/price-adjustment/), [IPv4 prices](https://docs.hetzner.com/general/infrastructure-and-availability/ipv4-pricing/), [backup billing](https://docs.hetzner.com/cloud/billing/faq/)

The old **$8–$10** and **$16–$18** monthly totals cannot be carried forward as production budgets. They omit confirmed restoration testing and may omit VM backups, monitoring, staging, backup retention, paid email, and additional capacity. Domain and Backblaze are annual-vs-monthly and USD-vs-EUR items; do not add currencies without an exchange assumption. Hetzner's listed English prices exclude VAT, and actual tax treatment and conversion fees depend on the billing entity and payment method. [Hetzner price schedule](https://docs.hetzner.com/general/infrastructure-and-availability/price-adjustment/)

## Inputs still needed for defensible scenarios

Record the Hetzner billing currency/location and tax treatment; selected VM sizes and count; whether a separate staging or standby instance is required; selected domain/renewal price; backups by dataset, retention, location and expected GB; any Volume, object storage, load balancer or external monitoring charges; email volume/provider; and measured transfer. Capacity and reliability decisions must precede low/expected/growth totals. Recheck every product price at checkout and date the resulting quote.
