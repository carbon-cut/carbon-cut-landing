# Carbon Cut domain vocabulary

This glossary records business terms used across Carbon Cut. It grows by domain area as those terms are clarified; the current entries concern Collectivity, the active flow.

**Carbon Cut**:
The overall application that can contain multiple offerings.

## Accounts

**User account**:
An identity used to sign in to Carbon Cut. It has an email address, first name, and last name, and may include an organisation.

## Collectivity

### Work and access

**Collectivity**:
An offering for local authorities within Carbon Cut. The GHG emissions inventory is one of its capabilities.

**Commune**:
A local authority covered by one project for a GHG emissions inventory. Each project covers one commune.

**Project**:
The GHG emissions inventory work for one commune.
_Avoid_: climate plan, when referring to inventory work.

**Subscription**:
An agreement for Collectivity services covering one or more communes.

**Subscription access**:
Permission to create a Project using the capacity of a Subscription.

**Service**:
A Collectivity capability purchased through a Subscription. A service can require activity data outside the GHG emissions inventory, such as data for an indicators or notation system.

### Emissions and boundaries

**Communal territory**:
The geographic area of a commune. A whole-territory inventory includes emissions sources beyond municipal assets.

**Inventory boundary**:
The part of a commune covered by an inventory: either its municipal assets or its whole territory. Municipal assets are included within the whole-territory boundary.

**Inventory year**:
A year covered by a GHG emissions inventory.

**Reference year**:
An inventory year chosen as the baseline for comparing emissions across years.

**GHG emissions inventory** (_inventaire des émissions de GES_):
The body of activity data and calculated greenhouse gas emissions and absorptions for a commune, its inventory boundary, and its inventory years.
_Avoid_: carbon balance (_bilan carbone_), municipal inventory, when referring to the full product scope.

**Inventory results**:
The calculated emissions and absorptions produced from a GHG emissions inventory.

**Municipal assets** (_patrimoine municipal_):
Municipal assets covered by the inventory, including buildings, the vehicle fleet, and public lighting.
_Avoid_: municipal heritage, patrimoine communal.
